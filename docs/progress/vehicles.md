# Agent 2 — Vehicles & Traffic (`vehicles`, `traffic`)

## Works

- `src/data/vehicles.ts`: `VEHICLES: Record<VehicleArchetypeId, VehicleArchetype>` for sedan, sports, muscle, suv, pickup, van, taxi, police, swat — name, `dims` (length/width/height, cabin start/length/height, hood/trunk heights, wheelbase, track, wheel radius/width), mass, `engineAccel`, `topSpeed` (sedan 38, sports 55, muscle 48, suv 36, pickup 37, van 33, taxi 37, police 46, swat 34 m/s), `brake`, `reverseSpeed`, `maxSteerRate`, `grip`, `handbrakeGrip`, `health: 1000`, `palette` (taxi yellow, police white/black, swat matte dark), `seats`, `chopValue` (as specified), `hornSound`. Also `isVehicleArchetypeId`, `DISTRICT_VEHICLE_MIX` (weighted archetype tables per district id + `default`), `TRAFFIC_DRIVER_TYPES`.
- `Vehicle` (`src/entities/Vehicle.ts`, `implements IVehicle`, every member incl. `velocity`, `heading`, `color`, `isPolice`, `length`, `driverEntity`, `setDriver`, `seatDoorPosition`, `explode`, `setSiren`, `teleport`). **Arcade fallback physics (SYSTEM_PROMPT §5 sanctioned)**: one yaw-locked Rapier dynamic cuboid (`Groups.VEHICLE`, filter ALL, contact events, linear damping 0.05, angular 2, `setEnabledRotations(false, true, false, true)`, collider bottom at the wheel contact so the body rests on the road). `fixedUpdate` projects the world linvel into the car frame, runs the pure `stepHandling` (`systems/vehicles/handling.ts`: throttle/brake/reverse, drag v², rolling resistance, top-speed falloff, steering authority that fades with speed, grip bleeding lateral velocity, handbrake grip for drift, airborne = no authority) and writes `setLinvel({x, y: keep, z})` + `setAngvel({0, yawRate, 0})`. Bodies without a controller and at rest are left alone so parked cars sleep. CCD on the player's car only. Grounded probe by raycast. Visual pitch to the road under the axles for the player's car (bridge ramps) without touching physics. Extra state via declaration merging on `VehicleStateInfo`: `wrecked`, `lightsOn`, `sirenOn`, `lateralSpeed`, `forwardSpeed`, `grounded`.
- Handling targets verified in `tests/vehicles/handling.test.ts` (11 vitest cases, pass): sedan > 12 m/s after 4 s WOT, all archetypes settle within 5 % of top speed, sports ≈ 55 m/s, sedan 90° corner at 15 m/s inside a city-block radius, handbrake + steer at 18 m/s peaks > 0.8 rad/s with > 2 m/s slide, muscle slides more than the sedan, uncontrolled cars coast to rest, light clock alternates axes with all-red gaps.
- `CarBuilder` (`src/gfx/CarBuilder.ts`) + `VehicleRenderer`: 14 `InstancedMesh`es (body, cabin, hood, trunk, front/rear bumper, 4 wheels, headlights, taillights, light bar, taxi sign) from unit box/cylinder geometries, scaled per instance from the archetype dims; per-instance color via `instanceColor` (body roles: paint / darkened by damage tier / near-black wrecks; glass; tires; lights). Headlights bright at night or when `L` toggled, taillights brighten while braking, reverse lights white; police bar alternates red/blue while the siren is on; taxi sign only on taxis. Live cars > 260 m from the camera are hidden by a zero-scale matrix. Buffers are only re-uploaded when written. Front wheels steer, all wheels spin with speed. Capacity 320 slots; farthest idle car is evicted when full.
- `VehicleSystem` (`src/systems/vehicles/`, `implements IVehiclesPeer`, `PRIORITY.vehicles`): pooled `Vehicle`s (`Pool`), `SpatialHash<Vehicle>` (cell 20) for `query`/`nearestEnterable`, `spawn`/`despawn` (emit `vehicle:spawned`/`vehicle:despawned`, private events in `events.ts`), parked spawner (`ParkedSpawner.ts`: `roadGraph.parkingSpots` thinned with 18 m spacing and per-kind chances to ≈ 1 per 40 m of street, cap 250, archetype mix by `world.districtAt`, spawned asleep, seeded `Random.fromString('leonida-parked')`), collision damage from `physics:contactStarted` + per-step Δv (`VehicleFx.ts`: ignores bumps < 1.5 m/s, `vehicle:collided`, `vehicle:damaged`, `vehicle.crash.light/medium/heavy`, camera shake when the player is inside), smoke < 300 hp, fire at ≤ 0 hp (`fire` particles, `vehicle.fire.loop`, 5 dps to occupants, traffic driver bails), explosion after 6 s (`game.damage?.explode(pos, 6, 100, 8000, source)` with a self-contained fallback that damages the player and vehicles within 6 m, `explosion` particles ×1.5, `vehicle.explosion`, `camera.shake(0.6, 0.7)`, occupants ejected/killed, `vehicle:exploded`, `crime:committed explosion` when the player caused it), wreck = black, undrivable, despawned 30 s later once > 60 m away. Bullets to the rear third count double (gas tank).
- Player control (`PlayerControl.ts`): `F` enter (0.8 s) / carjack (1.2 s: driver removed, `vehicle:carjacked {vehicle, position, heading, driverTypeId}`, `crime:committed carjack | steal_police_vehicle`), prompt `[F] Enter <name>` / `[F] Carjack <name>` pushed into `game.state.prompts`; requires player alive, not mounted, `!input.isBlocked`, `state.interior === null`; cancelled if the player walks > 5 m away. `F` exit → `player.dismount(doorPos)`, fall damage speed × 1.5 above 8 m/s, the car keeps rolling. Driving: `W/S` throttle / brake→reverse, `A/D` steer, `Space` handbrake, `H` horn (`vehicle:horn`), `L` lights toggle. `C` is a no-op (camera owns its distance). `game.state.vehicle = {id, speedKmh, health}` while driving. `vehicle.cameraTarget` (position, quaternion, velocity, `kind: 'vehicle'`, `size = length`) refreshed every frame. Adopts a car the player was mounted into by other systems.
- Audio (`VehicleAudio.ts`): `vehicle.engine.loop` for controlled cars within 60 m (`audio.engine(handle, rpm01, throttle, speed)` with a 5-gear rpm curve, position updated each frame, stopped out of range), `vehicle.skid.loop` while sliding, `vehicle.fire.loop`, `vehicle.siren.loop` via `setSiren` (+ `audio.siren`), `vehicle.door.open/close`, horn per archetype.
- Serialize/deserialize: the player's current vehicle (archetype, pose, color, health) is restored beside the player on load.
- `TrafficSystem` (`src/systems/traffic/`, `implements ITrafficPeer`, `PRIORITY.traffic`): keeps `density` (25) driven cars within 90–180 m of the player, spawned on `roadGraph.randomLanePointNear` outside the `THREE.Frustum` of `game.viewCamera`, ≥ 9 m from other cars, non-dirt edges, already rolling at 80 % of the limit; recycled beyond 220 m, or when stuck > 25 s and out of view. Cars the player enters/carjacks or whose driver bailed are dropped from the driven list and recycled once far away and empty. Light clock (`TrafficLights.ts`): 12 s green (last 2.5 s yellow) + 2 s all-red per axis, phase from `node.lightPhase`, only `hasLight` nodes, mirrored to the world lamps via `Props.setSignal`; `isGreen(nodeId, edgeId)` by edge axis. `panicNear(pos, radius)`: drivers within radius bail (brake to a stop, `setDriver(null)`, `game.peds?.spawn(driverTypeId, doorPos, {heading})`), triggered on loud `crime:committed` within 30 m and `vehicle:exploded` within 40 m.
- `TrafficDriver` (`implements VehicleDriver`): follows `lane.points` toward `lane.to` with a speed-scaled lookahead, picks a random outgoing edge at each node (no U-turns unless dead end, avoids dirt, keeps its lane index), target speed = `edge.speedLimit` × per-car variance, slows to 7 m/s for turns > 0.5 rad, car-following via the shared SpatialHash (22 m probe, matches speed, stops 4 m behind), waits for the player standing in the lane, stops at the stop line on red (yellow when it can still stop; commits when within 4 m at > 6 m/s), honks when blocked > 3 s (≤ 1 per 4 s, `vehicle:horn`), reroutes (lane change or different turn) after 8 s. Traffic drivers are virtual: `vehicle.driverEntity` is `null` for traffic; `vehicle.driverTypeId` ∈ `['business','worker','tourist','beachgoer']` is carried in the `vehicle:carjacked` event and used for the fleeing ped on panic.
- Autotests: `src/debug/autotests/vehicles.ts`, `src/debug/autotests/traffic.ts` (see How to test). Both drive the game only through `game.debug.run` and `game.input`.
- `npm run typecheck`: zero errors in owned files. `npx vitest run tests/vehicles`: 11/11 pass.

## Cut or deferred

- No raycast-per-wheel suspension: the arcade fallback is used throughout (design decision A). Bodies are yaw-locked so cars never roll over; bridge ramps are handled by Rapier's y velocity plus a visual-only pitch for the player's car.
- Traffic drivers have no visible ped inside the car (the driver is virtual until a carjack/panic spawns one via `game.peds`). Passengers are not simulated.
- Enter animation is a timed wait (0.8 s / 1.2 s), the player does not walk to the door.
- `C` (camera distance cycling) is intentionally a no-op; the camera system owns orbit/distance.
- Traffic does not yield at unlit intersections (first come, first served) and does not overtake; multi-lane roads only get lane changes when a car is blocked > 8 s.
- Vehicle-vs-ped damage is left to the peds system (it listens to `physics:contactStarted`), as agreed.
- No separate traffic autotest for the "no car stationary > 60 s" criterion at full length: the test budget uses a 20 s idle limit via `traffic.stalled` (stricter than the 60 s spec).

## Known bugs

- The arcade model uses a single ground probe under the chassis center: a car with its nose over a kerb edge may briefly report `grounded = false` (no steering authority) for a couple of frames.
- Traffic cars stopped nose-to-tail at a red light restart with a small lag chain (each waits for the gap ahead to open), which can leave the last cars of a long queue catching the next red.
- Collision damage uses per-step Δv of the vehicle, so hitting a sleeping parked car damages the moving car more than the parked one until the parked body wakes (one step later it also takes its share).
- Explosion fallback (without `game.damage`) does not ragdoll or damage peds — only the player and nearby vehicles.
- `Vehicle.ts` is 430 lines (slightly over the ~400 guideline); FX and player control are already split out.

## How to test

- Isolated run: `?systems=world,player,camera,vehicles,traffic` (`vehicles` alone also works: no traffic, parked cars only).
- Autotests:
  - `&autotest=vehicles` — spawns a sedan 5 m ahead on the longest flat street, `enterNearest`, holds `KeyW` 4 s → speed > 12 m/s and within 6 m of a lane point, `Space`+`KeyA` → |yaw rate| > 0.8 rad/s, `explodeCurrent` → `vehicle:exploded`, health ≤ 0, wrecked, player ejected, `game.state.vehicle === null`; spawn suv → enter → `exit` → player on foot, alive, no driver, no console errors.
  - `&autotest=traffic` — stands at the tightest cluster of three lit intersections, waits 15 s → `traffic.count` within ±6 of the target; within 60 s at all 3 sampled light nodes a car stops (< 0.5 m/s) on red within 12 m of the node; `traffic.stalled:20 === 0` throughout (no car idles > 20 s with a green light and a clear road); `traffic.panic` removes every driver within 60 m; no console errors.
- Keys: `F` enter/carjack/exit, `W/S` throttle/brake-reverse, `A/D` steer, `Space` handbrake, `H` horn, `L` lights.
- Debug actions: `vehicles.spawn:<archetype>:<x>:<z>[:<heading>]` (returns id), `vehicles.spawnHere:<archetype>`, `vehicles.enterNearest`, `vehicles.exit`, `vehicles.explodeCurrent`, `vehicles.count`, `vehicles.state`, `vehicles.despawnAll`, `traffic.setDensity:<n>`, `traffic.count` (`{count, target, abandoned}`), `traffic.panic`, `traffic.lights` (first 8 lit nodes → green axis / yellow), `traffic.stuck`, `traffic.stalled[:<seconds>]`.
- Unit tests: `npx vitest run tests/vehicles`.

## Integration requests

- None required for the code to run. Contracts consumed as-is: `IVehicle`, `IVehiclesPeer`, `ITrafficPeer`, `VehicleDriver`, `Events` (`vehicle:*`, `crime:committed`, `physics:contactStarted`), `Props.setSignal(nodeId, nsGreen, yellowAxis)`, `RoadGraph.randomLanePointNear/nearestLanePoint/parkingSpots`, `Player.mount/dismount/applyDamage`, `Camera.shake`.
- Peds engineer: subscribe to `vehicle:carjacked {vehicle, position, heading, driverTypeId}` and spawn the fleeing driver at `position`; traffic panic already spawns one itself through `game.peds.spawn` when your system is loaded.
- Police engineer: `vehicle.setDriver(new PoliceDriver())` + `vehicle.setSiren(true)` on a `vehicles.spawn('police', ...)` car is all that is needed; `TrafficSystem` automatically drops a car from its driven list when its driver is no longer a `TrafficDriver`.
- UI engineer: `game.state.vehicle` (`{id, speedKmh, health}`) is published every frame while driving for a speedometer/health bar; `game.state.prompts` carries `{key: 'F', text}` for enter/carjack.
